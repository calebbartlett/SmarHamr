SmarHamr Workbench

A unified way to explore, edit, and understand Perchance profiles — now called assets.

What are assets?

An asset is a reusable profile that defines behavior, personality, rules, or context. Characters, worlds, interaction profiles, and system personas are all assets. Workbench treats them the same so you can build anything without switching modes.

Why not “characters”?

Perchance calls every profile a character, even when it isn’t one. Workbench uses the term “asset” because it reflects how the data actually works. Assets are neutral — they can become characters, environments, scenes, groups of people, village, a ship, worlds, computer systems, or anything you design.

How does this relate to Perchance?

When you import a Perchance export, all profiles become assets in Workbench. Nothing changes in your data — only the way you understand and interact with it. Workbench helps you think in broader terms so you can build more powerful systems.
